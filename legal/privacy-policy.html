1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account creation via Apple Sign-In (iOS): When you sign in with Apple, we receive your email address and, if you choose to share it, your full name from Apple. Apple may provide a private relay email address if you opt to hide your email.
  • Profile information: Display name, bio, and profile photo. Your profile photo is uploaded from your device's photo library only when you explicitly choose to set or update it.
  • App activity: Movies and TV shows you log, your watch status, personal ratings, written reviews, comments, and custom lists you create.
  • Social activity: Accounts you choose to follow or block.
  • Feedback and bug reports: If you submit a bug report or feedback through the App, we collect the message you write along with device information (brand, model, operating system name and version, and app version) to help diagnose issues.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics: We use PostHog to understand how the App is used. PostHog collects your user ID, email address, and behavioral events such as which screens you visit, when you create or delete a review, when you search (including the search query and the number of results returned), and other in-app actions. A full list of event types is available on request.
  • Crash reports: We use Sentry to automatically detect and log app crashes and errors. Sentry captures your user ID, email address, device/OS information, and stack traces from crash events.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your precise location, phone number, payment information, or contacts. We do not use advertising trackers or sell advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To provide core app features: logging media, writing reviews, browsing other users' public lists, and following other accounts.
  • To detect, investigate, and fix bugs and crashes (Sentry).
  • To understand how the App is used and improve its features over time (PostHog).
  • To respond to your feedback, bug reports, or support requests.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect the safety of users.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: The third-party services listed above receive data solely to provide their respective functions for Talkie.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request (e.g., a court order or subpoena).
  • Protection of rights: We may disclose information when we believe it is necessary to prevent fraud, protect safety, or enforce our Terms of Service.

5. User-Generated Content

Content you post — including reviews, comments, and public lists — is visible to other Talkie users. If you set your account or lists to private, that content is only visible to you and users you approve. You can delete your content at any time from within the App.

If you report another user's content, the report (including the reported content and your account identifier) is reviewed by us and is not shared beyond that moderation context.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account through the App (Profile → Settings → Delete Account), your account is soft-deleted — your content is hidden from other users and your account can be restored for up to 30 days after deletion by contacting support@gettalkie.app.

To request permanent deletion of all your data, contact support@gettalkie.app. We will permanently remove your personal information within 30 days of a verified request.

Anonymized, aggregated analytics data (in PostHog) and error logs (in Sentry) may be retained beyond account deletion as they are no longer linked to your identity.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access and update: You can view and edit your profile at any time within the App.
  • Account deletion: You can delete your account from Profile settings.
  • Permanent data deletion: Email support@gettalkie.app to request permanent deletion.
  • Photo access: You can revoke the App's access to your photo library at any time in your device's Settings app. This will only prevent future avatar uploads; it will not delete an existing avatar.
  • Data deletion requests: You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting support@gettalkie.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Talkie is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact support@gettalkie.app.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale: We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out is required.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise these rights, contact support@gettalkie.app. We will respond to verified requests within 45 days.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and Supabase's Row Level Security policies that restrict access to your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Users

Talkie is operated from the United States. By using the App, you understand that your information will be processed and stored in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country of residence.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by posting a notice in the App or by sending an email to the address associated with your account before the changes take effect. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
